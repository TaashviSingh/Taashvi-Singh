Case Study

Cohyve

A creative platform that pairs businesses with the right design talent, and lets AI handle the messy bits of the process in between.

Project Type
Product Design · AI Platform
Focus
Product UX · Design System · AI Agent Interface
Duration
2024
Objective
Finding good design help is harder than it should be. Teams lose time chasing the right talent and still end up with work that doesn't hold together. Cohyve set out to close that gap: an ecosystem that matches businesses with vetted designers, backed by a proper design system and an AI agent that carries the busywork so the creative process actually keeps moving.
